Privacy Policy

Hope Resuscitated · Effective October 1, 2026

Hope Resuscitated is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to overdose prevention, based in St. Francisville, Louisiana. This policy explains what information we collect through our website (hope-resuscitated.org) and our social media tools, including our Facebook and Instagram integration, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us — such as your name, email address, or message when you contact us, sign up for updates, or volunteer.
  • Social media information — our app connects only to Hope Resuscitated's own Facebook Page and Instagram account. Through Meta's platform, it can access content published on those accounts and public engagement with it, such as comments, messages sent to our accounts, and basic public profile information (name and profile picture) of people who interact with us.
  • Basic website data — standard technical information such as browser type and pages visited, which may be collected by our hosting provider.

How we use information

  • To publish and schedule posts on our own Facebook Page and Instagram account.
  • To read and respond to comments and messages sent to our accounts.
  • To share overdose-prevention resources and answer questions about our programs.
  • To understand, in aggregate, how our outreach is performing.

We do not sell, rent, or trade personal information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Sharing

We share information only with service providers that help us run our website and social media tools (for example, Meta and our hosting provider), when required by law, or with your permission.

Retention and security

We keep information only as long as needed for the purposes above, and we use reasonable safeguards to protect it. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children

Our app and website are not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Data deletion instructions

You can ask us to delete any information we hold about you, including data received through Facebook or Instagram:

  1. Email Team@hope-resuscitated.org with the subject line "Data Deletion Request".
  2. Include your name and the Facebook or Instagram account name you used to interact with us.
  3. We will confirm your request and delete your information within 30 days.

You can also remove our app's access at any time in Facebook under Settings & privacy → Settings → Apps and websites.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows when it was last changed.
